Privacy Policy

This is short because the app does little: canta collects no personal data, transmits nothing and has no servers.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Microphone

canta needs the microphone to work: it is a singing trainer, and its only purpose is to detect the pitch of your voice in real time so it can tell you whether the note is in tune.

About that audio:

  • It is analysed on your device, as you sing, and discarded immediately.
  • It is never recorded to a file. Writing audio to disk is explicitly disabled in the app's code.
  • It is never transmitted to any server, ours or a third party's.
  • It is never shared with anyone.

The only things that survive the analysis are numbers — the detected pitch and the loudness — used in that instant to draw the screen and compute your score.

You can revoke the permission in your device settings at any time. Without it the app cannot hear you, so the exercises cannot be corrected.

What is stored, and where

canta stores the following only in your device's local storage:

  • Your measured vocal range.
  • Which lessons you have passed and your best score in each.
  • A history of your recent sessions: date, score, average deviation in cents and duration.
  • Your points, level and achievements.
  • Your preferences: language, note names, reference tuning and whether the guide tone plays.

This information never leaves your device. It is not synced, not backed up to our servers, and not tied to any identity: there are no accounts and no user identifiers.

What there isn't

  • No accounts or sign-up. We never ask for an email, name or phone number.
  • No analytics and no usage measurement.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No third-party trackers.
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos or device identity.

External links

The app links to naeku.io and ytrocket.com, external services for distributing and promoting music. If you open them you leave canta, and those sites' own privacy policies apply. canta sends them no information about you when you do.

Children

canta collects no data from anyone, children included. It is suitable for all ages and has no social features, chat or user-generated content.

How to delete your data

Two ways, both immediate:

  • In the app: "Your voice" → "Delete my progress". This removes lessons, scores, history, points and achievements.
  • By uninstalling the app: everything stored is deleted, including your vocal range and your preferences.

There is no need to write to us: since there are no servers and no copies, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If the app ever begins collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that change reaches a published version, and the date above will say so.
